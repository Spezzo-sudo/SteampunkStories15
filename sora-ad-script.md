# Sora Video Ad Script - Äther-Imperium: Chroniken des Dampfs

## Video Concept
30-second cinematic teaser showcasing the steampunk MMO strategy game with dramatic visuals transitioning from macro galaxy view to micro settlement details.

---

## Sora Prompt (English)

```
A cinematic 30-second video advertisement for a steampunk MMO strategy game.

Opening shot (0-5s): Camera slowly zooms into a vast hexagonal galaxy map floating in brass-framed Victorian ether space, with glowing amber energy lines connecting 3000+ star systems. Steam particles drift through the void. Warm sepia and bronze color palette.

Transition (5-10s): Camera dive-bombs toward one glowing hexagonal region, passing through swirling steam clouds and gear mechanisms. The hex grid becomes visible as we approach, showing 19 interconnected regions with ornate Victorian borders.

Mid sequence (10-20s): Camera glides over a detailed hexagonal territory showing steampunk settlements with brass towers, rotating gears, and steam vents. Airship convoys with propellers move between tiles. Resource nodes glow with Orichalkum crystals. Quick cuts show: a building under construction with scaffolding and gears, a research laboratory with blueprints and steam gauges, alliance banners waving.

Finale (20-28s): Pull back to reveal the massive interconnected empire, with fleet movements creating light trails between systems. Multiple players' territories light up across the galaxy. Steam pressure gauges rise.

End card (28-30s): Logo materializes from brass gears and steam: "Äther-Imperium: Chroniken des Dampfs" with tagline fading in below.

Style: Cinematic quality, dramatic lighting with warm amber/bronze tones, realistic steam and particle effects, intricate Victorian-era brass machinery details, strategic game interface elements subtly integrated, epic orchestral atmosphere implied through visual rhythm.
```

## Key Visual Elements to Emphasize

1. **Hexagonal Grid System** - Central visual motif throughout
2. **Steampunk Aesthetic** - Brass, gears, steam, Victorian architecture
3. **Scale** - From galaxy (3000+ systems) to individual settlements
4. **Strategic Depth** - Fleets moving, resources flowing, territories expanding
5. **MMO Elements** - Multiple player territories, alliance banners, interconnected empire
6. **Signature Resources** - Orichalkum crystals glowing amber/orange
7. **Steam Pressure (Kesseldruck)** - Gauges, vents, energy visualization
